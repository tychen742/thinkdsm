# Book Plan — Think DSM (thinkdsm.org)

## Title and Publication

- **Title**: Think Data Science & Management
- **URL**: thinkdsm.org
- **Format**: Jupyter Book (open access, online)

## Audience

- College students in an introductory data science and management course
- No assumed programming or data science background
- Application context: business and management

## Goals

- Introduce data science concepts through a business and management lens
- Build foundational Python, data manipulation, visualization, and statistical reasoning skills
- Prepare students to work with real-world datasets in a professional context

## Scope

Five parts:

| Part | Topic | Chapters |
|------|-------|----------|
| I | Fundamentals | 01 Intro to DS, 02 Python Basics |
| II | Working with Data | 03 NumPy, 04 Pandas |
| III | Data Visualization | 05 Visualization Overview, 06 Matplotlib, 07 Seaborn |
| IV | Inferential Statistics | 08 Probability & Stats, 09 Hypothesis Testing, 10 Two Samples, 11 Estimation, 12 Regression |
| V | Machine Learning | 13 Multiple Regression, 14 Classification, 15 Clustering |

Appendices cover tooling, cheatsheets, and Jupyter setup.

See `_toc.yml` for the authoritative notebook order.

## Out of Scope

- Advanced ML (deep learning, neural networks)
- Software engineering practices
- Database systems
