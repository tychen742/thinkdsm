# Chapter 08 Organization

## Chapter Role

Chapter 08 prepares students for hypothesis testing by connecting probability, distributions, summaries, and sampling variability.

## Learning Goals

Students should be able to:

1. Describe probability as a model for uncertain outcomes.
2. Summarize distributions using center, spread, and shape.
3. Interpret empirical distributions in management contexts.
4. Explain why samples vary from one draw to another.
5. Use simulation to reason about sampling variability.
